As Director of Marketing and Strategy, I designed for the largest student-led conference for women in tech in the world.


Alongside my co-director, I led the team responsible for carrying out various year-long initiatives, including executing attendee-focused campaigns, social media content creation, and designing the visual image of WECode.

Who is WECode?

WECode (Women Engineers Code) is an annual conference organized by undergraduate women at Harvard University. While the conference is hosted in the spring, WECode maintains a year-round community, with workshops and events for distinguished Tech Fellows, mentorship for students, and resources shared through regular newsletters and social media content.

One of the first questions I wanted to answer when I began my role as Director of Marketing and Strategy for the 2026 Conference in May 2025 was "Who is WECode?"

Contrary to WECode's name, it's not an organization that codes. My mission was to showcase the bigger image of what our community stands for; I wanted to highlight how at WECode, we're confident that together, we can do anything.

Creating a Long-lasting Brand

The 2026 co-chairs of WECode had a major concern: each year, WECode seemed to reinvent the wheel. In past conferences, a new mascot was designed annually and tended to lack consistency across the organization, rarely used except on the conference t-shirts.

WECode needed a mascot that was permanent, unique, and representative of the organization's mission.

Women in technology are resilient, they constantly adapt to their environments and grow stronger in the face of challenges. These characteristics are all part of an axolotl, a creature known for its ability to regenerate body parts, including limbs and even parts of its brain.

In addition, the commonly-known type of axolotl seamlessly fit in with WECode's pink palette, making it a perfect choice for the organization's visual identity.
