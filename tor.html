Personal spec animation

The Haunting of Lady Death

By Maddie Martinez · Tor Books · Coming February 9, 2027

  • Built from the finished cover: I cut it into layers in Photoshop and animated them in After Effects, with a slow push-in, flickering candlelight, and characters that come to life through the cover.
  • Animated by hand ✨ No generative AI was used.
  • Short-form videos like these could be used to do cover reveals on social platforms such as Instagram and TikTok.
  • When brainstorming the project, I decided to browse through Tor's social media posts to find a book that didn't have a lot of promo yet but caught my eye. I enjoyed getting to work with the cover illustration and learning more about the book through this process.

Tools: Photoshop, After Effects

Cover art and title © Tor Books, Maddie Martinez (author), Mary Metzger (Illustrator), and Lesley Worrell (Designer). This is a personal spec project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tor.
